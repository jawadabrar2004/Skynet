SNAP assistant

Hungry? What do you want to eat or buy today?

You can type in 2 bags of milk, bags of chips, butter, tissue paper.

You can also name a meal, like “I want to make a burger,” and I’ll list the groceries to make it.

Based on federal USDA SNAP rules as of September 2026. Connecticut has no extra soda or candy ban. Rules can change, so check CT DSS for official updates. Want to know where to shop? Find SNAP/EBT stores near you.
